Privacy Policy

CarrierConnect360 · Operated by Trucking Compliance Services · Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trucking Compliance Services ("Company," "we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and shares information through CarrierConnect360 (the "Service"). It applies to visitors to our website, trial and paid Customers (trucking/logistics companies), and the individual users of a Customer's account — including office staff, drivers, and business partners ("Users").

Because the Service is used by trucking companies to manage their own drivers and staff, this policy describes two roles: Customer — the trucking company that controls the account and decides what data to input (acts as data controller for its own drivers' and staff's information); and Us / the Company — we process that data on the Customer's behalf to provide the Service (acting as a data processor/service provider).

If you are a driver or staff member whose information was entered by your employer (a Customer), your employer controls that data; you may need to contact them directly for certain requests, though we will assist as required by law.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account & business information (from Customers/office users): Name, email, phone, company name, business address, EIN/tax ID (where provided for 1099/check purposes), login credentials, subscription/billing information.

B. Driver and personnel information (entered by Customers): Name, contact info, license/CDL details, employment/application data, road test and qualification file records, drug & alcohol Clearinghouse consent records (including a driver's last-4 SSN digits and e-signature/consent audit trail used to verify identity for Clearinghouse-related queries), pay rate and settlement history, uploaded documents (e.g., medical cards, MVR, background check results), and photos (e.g., BOL/POD, vehicle photos).

C. Operational data: Load, dispatch, and routing information; vehicle and fuel records; invoices and broker information; expense and accounting records; IFTA worksheets; uploaded ELD/GPS files.

D. Automatically collected data: IP address, browser/device type, log data, and usage data collected via standard web technologies when you use the Service or visit our website.

E. Support & communications: Messages sent to our AI support assistant, live chat, or support email/ticket system.

We do not intentionally collect full Social Security numbers through the Service's standard workflows; Clearinghouse-related identity verification uses only the last four digits, consistent with common industry practice, but you should verify this matches your compliance requirements.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service (including multi-tenant data isolation between Customers);
  • Process driver pay, checks, invoices, and 1099 tax forms as directed by the Customer;
  • Power AI-assisted features (rate confirmation reading, the in-app support assistant);
  • Send transactional emails (e.g., password resets, expiration reminders, settlement notifications, invoices);
  • Provide customer support (AI assistant, live chat, human support tickets);
  • Maintain security, detect fraud or abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • Improve and develop the Service;
  • With your consent or as permitted by law, communicate with you about product updates or send information you've requested (e.g., a demo).

We do not sell Customer Data or driver personal information to third parties.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Sub-processors / infrastructure providers — cloud application hosting, the database, and cloud object storage used to host the Service and store uploaded files/backups.
  • AI provider — rate-confirmation text extraction and the support assistant are powered by a third-party large language model provider. Content you submit to these features (e.g., an uploaded rate confirmation, or a support question, which may reference your own scoped account data) is sent to that provider to generate a response. It is not used by us to train models for other customers.
  • Email delivery provider — for transactional and account emails.
  • Live chat provider — powers the in-app and website live-chat widget; messages sent through it are processed by that provider.
  • Mapping/geolocation provider (if enabled) — for address lookups, mileage, and navigation features.
  • Payment processor (once billing is enabled) — to process subscription payments; we do not store full card numbers ourselves.
  • Legal & safety — if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Company, our Customers, or others.
  • Business transfers — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy or a successor policy.

Within the Service: a Customer's data is isolated from other Customers. Only authorized users within a Customer's own account (based on their assigned role) can see that Customer's data. Platform administrators (us) can access data as needed to provide support, troubleshoot, or as required by law.

4. AI Features

CarrierConnect360 uses AI (large language model) technology for two features: (1) Rate confirmation reading — extracts structured details (addresses, dates, stop info) from an uploaded document to save data entry time; and (2) Support assistant — answers questions about how to use the app, and can reference the asking Customer's own account data to give relevant answers. It is scoped so it cannot access another Customer's data.

Content submitted to these features is sent to a third-party AI provider for processing. AI output may contain errors and should be reviewed by a human before being relied upon, especially for compliance-sensitive or financial information.

5. Data Retention

We retain Customer Data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period after cancellation to allow data export, unless a longer period is required for legal, tax, or regulatory recordkeeping purposes (for example, DOT/FMCSA-related driver qualification records, which carriers are typically required to retain for specific periods under 49 CFR Part 391 — Customers remain responsible for meeting those retention obligations independent of the Service).

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, role-based permissions, and cloud backups, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and role, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • If you are a Customer (business account holder): you can access and export most of your data directly within the app, or contact us for assistance.
  • If you are a driver or staff member added by a Customer: please contact your employer (the Customer) first, as they control that account; we will support their request as required by law and our role as service provider.
  • To make a request directly to us, contact info@carrierconnect360.com.

As a California-based company, we are mindful of the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). Depending on your role and residency, you may have rights to know what personal information is collected, request deletion or correction, and opt out of certain sharing. Residents of other states or jurisdictions may have similar rights under their own laws. Contact us at the email above to make a request, and have this section reviewed by an attorney to confirm exactly which rights and disclosures apply to your business before relying on it.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Our website and app may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for login sessions, security, and basic functionality, and the live-chat widget may use its own cookies. We do not currently use third-party advertising trackers on the app itself; UTM parameters used for lead-source tracking are read from the URL at signup/demo-request time, not tracked via invasive third-party ad pixels.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use by adults (18+) operating or working for a trucking/logistics company. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. International Data

The Service and its infrastructure are operated from and intended for use in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted via an in-app notice or email. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data: info@carrierconnect360.com
