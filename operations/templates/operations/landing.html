Frequently asked questions

How much does CarrierConnect360 cost?

$100/month for 1–5 trucks, plus $20/month for each additional truck. All features are included at every size — nothing is locked behind a higher tier. A 7-day free trial is available with no credit card required.

What does CarrierConnect360 include?

Dispatch and load management, a driver mobile app, driver pay and check printing, broker invoicing, accounting and partner ledgers, IFTA fuel-tax worksheets, FMCSA driver qualification file tracking, and 1099 contractor tax generation — all in one multi-tenant platform.

Is CarrierConnect360 suitable for owner-operators and small fleets?

Yes. Pricing scales from a single truck up through larger fleets, and every company's data is kept separate and private. Owner-operators can be paid as a business (with EIN) directly from the platform.

Does CarrierConnect360 help with FMCSA compliance?

CarrierConnect360 organizes driver qualification files, road test records, and consent workflows aligned to 49 CFR Part 391. It's a recordkeeping tool, not legal or compliance advice — carriers should still have a qualified DOT compliance professional review their program.

Can I try CarrierConnect360 before paying?

Yes. New accounts include a 7-day free trial with full access to every feature, and no credit card is required to start.
