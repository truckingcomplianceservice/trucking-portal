How this works

Fleet MPG = total miles ÷ total gallons. For each state: taxable gallons = miles ÷ fleet MPG; net gallons = taxable gallons − gallons bought there; tax = net gallons × that state's rate. A positive number means you owe that state; a negative number is a credit.

You must enter: miles driven in each state, and each state's current fuel tax rate (rates change every quarter — get them from your IFTA filing packet or your base state's rate sheet). Pulled automatically: gallons purchased per state (from your Fuel records — make sure each fuel transaction has its state set).

This is a worksheet to help you file — it is not an official return and does not submit to any state. Please verify figures before filing, or share them with your accountant.
