📣 How to track your Meta & Google ads

Add UTM tags to the link in your ads so every lead records which ad brought them. Point your ads to:
https://www.carrierconnect360.com/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=spring_launch&utm_content=ad1
  • utm_source = facebook or google (the platform)
  • utm_medium = cpc / paid
  • utm_campaign = your campaign name
  • utm_content = which ad (to compare ads)
When someone signs up or requests a demo, the lead here shows that source/campaign automatically — so you see which ads actually produce customers.
